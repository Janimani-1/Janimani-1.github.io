Privacy Policy – Penalty Kings

Developer: Jani Mani Games · Last update: 16 November 2026

What we store

The game stores your progress (balls, diamonds, unlocked stages and players, cups, settings, chosen name and face picture) only on your device.

Your face picture

If you take or choose a picture for your player face, it is reduced to a small image and kept only on your device. It is not uploaded.

Online play and records

When online matches and world records are switched on, your player name, face picture, country flag, points and ball count are sent to our game server (Google Firebase) so other players can see them. Nothing else is shared.

Ads

Rewarded ads are provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may use device identifiers to show and measure ads. See Google's ad policy.

Purchases

Diamond packs are bought through Google Play. We never see your payment details. Balls and diamonds are virtual items for the game only and cannot be exchanged for real money.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13.

Delete your data

Uninstalling the game deletes all data on your device. To delete online data write to jani.mani1359@gmail.com.
